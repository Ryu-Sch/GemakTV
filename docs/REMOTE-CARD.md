# Your TV

*Print this page and keep it next to the TV.*

---

## To watch TV

**Turn the TV on.** It starts by itself on the channel you watched last.

## To change the channel

Press **▲** or **▼**.

Or press the channel's **number** (1, 2, 3 …).

## To make it louder or softer

Press **Volume +** or **Volume −**.

## To see all the channels

Press **OK** in the middle of the arrows.
Choose a channel with **▲ ▼** and press **OK**.

Press **BACK** to go back to the TV.

---

## If a channel says *"not available right now"*

Nothing is broken. The TV keeps trying by itself.
Press **▲** or **▼** to watch another channel meanwhile.

## If it says *"No internet connection"*

Wait a little. The TV starts again by itself when the internet is back.

## If someone asks you to update the TV

1. Press **OK**.
2. Press **▶**.
3. Choose **Check For Updated Channel List** or **Check For Updates** and press **OK**.
4. When it is done, press **OK**.
